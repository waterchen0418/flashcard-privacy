# 隱私權政策 / Privacy Policy

最後更新 / Last updated: 2026-10-20

## 繁體中文

FlashCard（以下稱「本 App」）重視你的隱私。本政策說明本 App 如何處理資料。

### 我們收集的資料

**本 App 不收集你的個人資料，也不會把你新增的單字傳送到任何伺服器。**

- 你新增的單字、翻譯、例句、標籤，以及學習紀錄與設定，都只儲存在你的裝置上。
- 本 App 沒有帳號系統，不需要註冊或登入。
- 單字發音使用 iOS 內建的語音功能，在裝置上處理。

### 匿名使用統計

為了了解哪些功能實際被使用、進而改善 App，我們使用 [TelemetryDeck](https://telemetrydeck.com)（一家以隱私為導向的分析服務）收集匿名的使用統計，內容包含：

- 你執行的動作，例如完成一次學習、使用聆聽模式或文章生成、切換語言、開啟升級頁或購買訂閱。
- 基本裝置資訊，例如裝置型號、系統版本與 App 版本。
- 以安裝為單位產生的匿名識別碼，用來計算不重複的使用者數。

這些資料無法用來識別你的身分，也不會與你的其他資料連結。**我們不會記錄你新增的單字內容。** 本 App 不使用廣告識別碼（IDFA）、不進行跨 App 或跨網站追蹤，也不會與廣告商或資料掮客分享任何資料。

### 訂閱與付款

Pro 訂閱由 Apple 的 App Store 處理。付款資訊由 Apple 管理，我們不會取得你的信用卡或付款資料。你可以隨時在 App Store 的帳號設定中管理或取消訂閱。

### 資料刪除

你的單字與學習紀錄只存在你的裝置上，刪除本 App 即會移除。匿名使用統計無法連結到個人，因此無法依個人要求刪除。

### 兒童隱私

本 App 不會刻意收集任何人的個人資料，包含 13 歲以下兒童。

### 政策變更

若本政策有變更，我們會更新本頁面並修改上方的「最後更新」日期。

### 聯絡我們

如有任何問題，請來信：**tuiroulo.app@gmail.com**

## English

FlashCard (the "App") respects your privacy. This policy explains how the App handles data.

### Data We Collect

**The App does not collect your personal data and never sends the words you add to any server.**

- The words, translations, examples and tags you add, along with your study progress and settings, are stored only on your device.
- The App has no account system. No sign-up or login is required.
- Word pronunciation uses the speech feature built into iOS and is processed on your device.

### Anonymous Usage Statistics

To understand which features are actually used and to improve the App, we use [TelemetryDeck](https://telemetrydeck.com), a privacy-focused analytics service, to collect anonymous usage statistics. This includes:

- Actions you take, such as completing a study session, using Listen Mode or Story Builder, changing the language, opening the upgrade screen, or subscribing.
- Basic device information, such as device model, OS version and App version.
- An anonymous per-installation identifier, used to count unique users.

This data cannot be used to identify you and is not linked to any other data about you. **We do not record the content of the words you add.** The App does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not share any data with advertisers or data brokers.

### Subscriptions and Payments

Pro subscriptions are handled by Apple's App Store. Payment information is managed by Apple; we never receive your credit card or payment details. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your App Store account settings.

### Data Deletion

Your words and study progress are stored only on your device, and deleting the App removes them. Anonymous usage statistics cannot be linked to an individual, so they cannot be deleted on an individual's request.

### Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

### Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.

If you have any questions, email: **tuiroulo.app@gmail.com**
