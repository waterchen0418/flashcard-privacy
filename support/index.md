# 支援 / Support

FlashCard 單字卡

## 繁體中文

**有問題請來信：[tuiroulo.app@gmail.com](mailto:tuiroulo.app@gmail.com)**
一個人開發，通常兩到三個工作天內回覆。也可以在 App 內「統計 → 右上角齒輪 → 聯絡客服」直接寄信。

### 免費版有什麼限制？

免費版最多可以存 20 個單字，每天可以生成 3 次文章，文章次數每天重置。訂閱 Pro 之後兩者都沒有上限。

### Pro 怎麼收費？

Pro 有月訂閱與年訂閱兩種，皆為自動續訂，透過你的 Apple 帳號扣款。除非在目前期間結束前至少 24 小時關閉自動續訂，否則會自動續訂。

### 怎麼取消訂閱？

訂閱由 Apple 管理。到 iPhone「設定 → 你的名字 → 訂閱」，或在 App 內「統計 → 齒輪 → 管理訂閱」。取消後，Pro 會用到目前期間結束。

### 換手機之後 Pro 不見了

在 App 內「統計 → 齒輪 → 還原購買」。只要是同一個 Apple 帳號，就會恢復。

### 換手機之後單字不見了

單字與學習紀錄只存在這台裝置上，不會上傳，所以目前無法在裝置之間轉移。刪除 App 也會一併刪除這些資料。

### 聽不到單字發音

請確認 iPhone 沒有切到靜音模式，並把音量調大。也可以在「統計 → 齒輪」確認「單字發音」已開啟。

### 我的單字會被上傳嗎？

不會。單字、翻譯、例句與學習紀錄都只存在你的裝置上，本 App 沒有帳號系統。我們只收集不含個人身分的匿名使用統計，詳見[隱私權政策](../)。

### 支援哪些裝置？

iOS 17 以上的 iPhone。

### 我要退款

退款由 Apple 處理。請到 [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) 提出申請。

## English

**Questions: [tuiroulo.app@gmail.com](mailto:tuiroulo.app@gmail.com)**
Built by one person. You'll usually hear back within two or three working days. You can also write from inside the app: Stats tab → gear icon → Contact Support.

### What does the free version include?

Up to 20 words and 3 article generations per day. The article count resets daily. Pro removes both limits.

### How is Pro billed?

Pro is a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription, charged to your Apple Account. It renews automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

### How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Go to iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or in the app: Stats → gear icon → Manage Subscription. After cancelling, Pro stays active until the end of the current period.

### I got a new phone and Pro is gone

In the app, go to Stats → gear icon → Restore Purchase. As long as you use the same Apple Account, it will come back.

### I got a new phone and my words are gone

Your words and study history are stored only on the device and are never uploaded, so they can't be moved between devices at the moment. Deleting the app also deletes them.

### I can't hear the pronunciation

Make sure your iPhone isn't in Silent mode and the volume is up. You can also check that Word Pronunciation is turned on under Stats → gear icon.

### Are my words uploaded anywhere?

No. Your words, translations, examples and study history stay on your device, and there is no account. We only collect anonymous usage statistics that don't identify you. See the [privacy policy](../).

### Which devices are supported?

iPhone running iOS 17 or later.

### I'd like a refund

Refunds are handled by Apple. Please request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
